Prologue: Technological Singularity

Friday, May 2, 2026 — History's Future

Not in the sci‑fi sense — no glowing orbs, no robot overlords, no dramatic takeover. The singularity is here, but visible only in the quiet, invisible acceleration of intelligence, computation, and data that underlies the substrate of life on Earth. It is evident in the widening gap between what humans can understand and what our machines can generate.

We are living through the most consequential transformation in the history of our species, and most people are too busy scrolling on their devices to notice.
